# User Guide — Obstacle Avoidance App

This app uses your iPhone or iPad camera to detect nearby obstacles and announce them aloud in real time. It is designed to work with **VoiceOver**, Apple's built-in screen reader.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Turn on VoiceOver

VoiceOver is what allows the app to speak obstacle warnings to you.

**To turn on VoiceOver:**
- Go to **Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver** and toggle it on.
- Or ask Siri: *"Turn on VoiceOver."*

> You can also set up the **Accessibility Shortcut** (triple-click the side button) to quickly toggle VoiceOver on and off. Go to **Settings → Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcut** and select VoiceOver.

### 2. Open the App

Launch the **Obstacle Avoidance** app. You will land on the **Home** tab, which contains basic instructions.

### 3. Go to the Camera Tab

Tap the **Camera** tab at the bottom of the screen. The app will begin detecting objects immediately using your device's camera.

Point the camera forward as you walk. The app will announce obstacles it detects, including:
- What the object is (e.g. *"Person"*, *"Chair"*, *"Car"*)
- Where it is relative to you (e.g. *"center"*, *"left"*, *"right"*)
- How far away it is (e.g. *"1.2 meters"*)

That's it — no account, no login, no setup required.

---

## Settings

Tap the **Settings** tab to adjust preferences.

### System Preferences

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Measurement Type** | Switch distance announcements between feet and meters |
| **User Height** | Set your height in inches so distance estimates are more accurate |
| **Haptic Feedback** | Toggle vibration feedback (if supported) |
| **Share Location** | Toggle location sharing |

### Emergency Contacts

You can store emergency contact information (name, phone number, address) in the app for quick reference. These are saved on your device only.

---

## Tips

- **Hold the device upright** and pointed slightly downward toward the path ahead for the best detection.
- **Announcements will not repeat constantly** — the app pauses briefly between warnings to avoid being overwhelming.
- **Distance readings require LiDAR.** If your device does not have a LiDAR sensor (iPhone 12 Pro and later, iPad Pro 2020 and later), the app will still detect objects but will not announce distances.
- If announcements stop, check that **VoiceOver is still enabled** in your Accessibility settings.

---

## Supported Devices

- iPhone or iPad running **iOS 16 or later**
- LiDAR sensor required for distance announcements (detection works on all supported devices)
